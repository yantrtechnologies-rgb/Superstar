# Postiz feature parity research

Observed 2026-11-17. Routes below are CadenceDesk targets. Verification is recorded per feature. The [documentation index](https://docs.postiz.com/llms.txt) and [Postiz repository](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app/tree/36d5fc7b3ac3f17178b1589cf7a7337523017a41) are the research references.

| Feature | Evidence | CadenceDesk surface | Phase | Verification or dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace and team access | [Team members](https://docs.postiz.com/general/settings/team) | /app, /app/[workspace]/settings, /login | 2 | Implemented and exercised in [identity Playwright tests](../tests/e2e/identity.spec.ts). Live Resend requires a verified sender and sending key. |
| Channel connection and reconnect | [Connect a channel](https://docs.postiz.com/general/channels/connect) | Channels | 3–4 | Discord, Telegram and Slack use operator bot configuration and workspace grants. LinkedIn member profiles use OAuth with encrypted credentials and conditional refresh. All connect through /app/[workspace]/channels. See [provider catalog](provider-catalog.md) and [provider tests](../apps/worker/test/publishing.integration.mjs). Live credentials and broader parity are pending. |
| Composer, per-channel overrides, threads and comments | [Composer](https://docs.postiz.com/general/composer/overview) | Compose | 3–4 | Documented. Format and reply behavior require per-provider evidence. |
| Calendar, filters, rescheduling and repeat posts | [Calendar](https://docs.postiz.com/general/calendar/overview), [scheduling](https://docs.postiz.com/general/composer/scheduling) | Calendar, posts | 3 | Agenda and scheduled publish implemented. See [workflow test](../apps/worker/test/publishing.integration.mjs). Calendar grids, rescheduling and repetition are pending. |
| Media library and editing | [Media library](https://docs.postiz.com/general/media-library) | Media | 3, 6 | Documented. Object storage and media workers required. |
| Client review and comments | [Approvals](https://docs.postiz.com/general/approvals) | Review, posts | 3, 5 | Expiring revocable review links, comments, client approval and revision invalidation implemented. Assignments remain pending. See [Playwright coverage](../tests/e2e/identity.spec.ts). |
| Client groups and posting slots | [Client groups](https://docs.postiz.com/general/channels/customers), [time slots](https://docs.postiz.com/general/channels/time-slots) | Channels, calendar | 5 | Groups, agenda filtering and shared weekly slots implemented. Group-limited membership remains pending. |
| Metrics and snapshots | [Analytics](https://docs.postiz.com/general/analytics) | Analytics | 5 | Telegram member count and date-filtered snapshots implemented through Temporal. Other provider metrics, post-level analytics and exports are pending. See [worker coverage](../apps/worker/test/publishing.integration.mjs). |
| AI, clipping, RSS, plugs, third-party imports and webhooks | [Documentation index](https://docs.postiz.com/llms.txt) | Studio, automations | 6 | Text and image jobs, server-side credit quotes, reservations and refunds, and RSS-to-draft are implemented. See [integration tests](../apps/worker/test/publishing.integration.mjs). Signed publication webhooks with persisted retries are available in /app/[workspace]/automations/webhooks. Live AI credentials, clipping and plugs are pending. |
| Sets, signatures, notifications and developer settings | [Settings](https://docs.postiz.com/general/settings/overview) | Settings | 5, 7 | Documented surfaces. Inventory exact permissions in authenticated app. |
| REST, onboarding links, SDK, CLI, MCP and automation clients | [Public API](https://docs.postiz.com/public-api/introduction), [documentation index](https://docs.postiz.com/llms.txt) | Settings and external clients | 7 | OAuth connection endpoint is narrower than the full connector picker. Verify embedding contracts and client access. |
| Post exchange and paid placements | [Repository README](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app) | Pending verified workflow | 7 | README describes it. Source walkthrough, payment dependency and live availability remain pending. |
| Hosted billing and AI usage | [Public pricing](https://postiz.com/pricing) | Pricing, billing, admin | 8 | Public plan evidence. Hosted enforcement requires authenticated testing. |
| Self-hosting | [Documentation index](https://docs.postiz.com/llms.txt) | Operator setup | 1, 9 | Documented track. Pinned deployment and environment files still need inspection. |
