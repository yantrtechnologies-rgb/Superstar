## Demo video script (3–4 minutes, English, no sound needed)

Record the whole browser window so the **address bar is always visible**. Use a Google account that is not the
project owner if you can (for example a second Gmail), and a YouTube channel you control.

1. **Show the app.** Open https://superstar.xolnet.in. Show the home page and the privacy policy link
   (https://superstar.xolnet.in/legal/privacy).
2. **Sign in with Google.** Go to https://superstar.xolnet.in/login → **Sign in with Google**. Show Google's account
   chooser and the consent screen (name, email, profile). You land in your Superstar workspace.
3. **Connect YouTube (show every Google screen slowly).** Channels → **Authorize or reconnect YouTube channel**.
   - Show the account chooser.
   - Show **"Google hasn't verified this app"** → Advanced → Go to xolnet.in (this screen is expected and must be in
     the video).
   - Show the permission screen listing the three YouTube permissions, pause on it for 3 seconds, click Continue.
   - Back in Superstar, show the YouTube channel name in the channel list (**youtube.readonly**).
4. **Upload a video (youtube.upload).** Posts → New post. Write a title/description, attach a short MP4, select the
   YouTube channel, click **Publish now**. Open the video on YouTube (or YouTube Studio) to show it was uploaded.
5. **Comments (youtube.force-ssl).** From another account, leave a comment on that video. In Superstar open **Inbox**,
   show the comment, click **Reply**, type a reply, **Send reply**. Open the video on YouTube and show the reply
   posted by your channel.
6. **User control.** Channels → show **Disconnect** for the YouTube channel.

Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, paste the link into **Data Access → YouTube link → Save**, then open the
Verification Center link above and click **Submit for verification**. Google replies by email to
xolnettechnologies@gmail.com (usually within a few days; answer any questions there).
